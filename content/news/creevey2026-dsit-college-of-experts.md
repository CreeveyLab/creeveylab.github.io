Chris has been appointed to the UK Department for Science, Innovation and Technology's new College of Experts, a network of independent specialists giving DSIT rapid, flexible access to leading scientific and technical expertise. He joins 71 members selected from nearly 1,200 applicants, launched formally at the Royal Society in London on 18 June 2026.
